# Medical Imaging Dataset Guide

The official course webpage contains a long list of public datasets suitable for final projects. This page organizes representative options by modality/task so students can choose more easily.

**Complete official list:**  
https://users.encs.concordia.ca/~hrivaz/courses/Med_Imag_444_2026/

## MRI

- BrainWeb MRI — simulation / brain MRI experiments
- BraTS — brain tumor MRI and segmentation
- WMH Segmentation — white matter hyperintensity segmentation
- OASIS — brain MRI
- BITE / RESECT — MRI + ultrasound for image-guided intervention / registration

## CT

- DIR-Lab — thoracic CT and registration
- NLST — chest CT
- TCIA Pancreas CT-82 — pancreas CT segmentation
- KiTS — kidney tumor CT
- Medical Segmentation Decathlon — multiple CT/MRI segmentation tasks
- LiTS — liver and tumor segmentation
- Beyond the Cranial Vault — abdominal CT

## Ultrasound

- Ultrasound RF/raw-data resources listed on the official course page
- Fetal head circumference dataset
- RESECT-SEG — intra-operative brain tumor ultrasound segmentation
- Breast ultrasound datasets
- TN3K — thyroid nodule segmentation
- CAMUS — 2D echocardiography
- EchoNet-Dynamic — cardiac ultrasound videos
- Pediatric brain ultrasound segmentation data
- Kidney ultrasound dataset

## X-ray / mammography

- SCR chest radiograph segmentation dataset
- INbreast digital mammography
- Curated Breast Imaging Subset of DDSM

## Retinal / OCTA

- ROSE retinal OCT-angiography vessel-segmentation dataset

## Endoscopy

- HyperKvasir — gastrointestinal endoscopy images/videos

## Choosing a dataset

Before committing to a dataset, check:

- Is the dataset publicly accessible to you?
- Is the license compatible with your project?
- Is the size manageable with your available hardware?
- Does it include the labels/annotations needed for your task?
- Is there an established baseline you can reproduce?
- Are the train/validation/test splits clearly defined?

For the source links and the full list of datasets recommended by the course, use the official course page linked above.
